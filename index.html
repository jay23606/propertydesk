Properties and account management are combined here. The Payment cell is light pink when no payment has been recorded, light green for a partial payment, and darker green once receipts reach that account's scheduled amount for the current calendar month. Only posted rent or installment receipts count; partial receipts count toward the total. Unpaid Due includes this calendar month's scheduled payment immediately and subtracts posted receipts. Tracking starts Oct 2026 for rentals, land contracts, and notes so earlier unrecorded installments are not assumed unpaid. Later unpaid installments carry forward.
